02 — Foundations

Typography

One squared grotesque, two widths. It shares the mark’s flat terminals and tight geometry, so headlines sit next to the logo without arguing with it.

Archivo
Primary family. Weights 400 / 500 / 600 / 700 / 800. Headlines, body, buttons, everything by default.
'Archivo', 'Helvetica Neue', Arial, sans-serif
Archivo Narrow
Eyebrows, kickers, tags, data labels — the condensed voice from the logo tagline. Never body copy.
'Archivo Narrow', 'Arial Narrow', Arial, sans-serif
If you have the licensed fonts
If the brand standards specify licensed faces — Klavika and Klavika Condensed, which the wordmark is drawn from — set those for print. Archivo is the web and desktop stand-in used throughout this system; check a headline side by side before swapping, as the metrics are close but not identical.

Sentence case for headlines
Title Case Reads Like An Announcement. Sentence case reads like a person. Caps are reserved for eyebrows and button labels: eyebrows track at 0.14em or more, buttons at 0.04em.
Floors
12px on screen, 12pt (16px at 96ppi) for print body copy. Anything smaller is a legal line, not information.
Two sizes per block
A heading and its body. Reach for weight and color to make a third level before you reach for a third size.
