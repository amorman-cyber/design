03 — Foundations

The mark

Four lockups cover every use. Pick by background, not by preference: two-color on white or a light photo, one-color where ink is limited, reversed on slate or a dark image. The green half of the logotype is artwork, so it is exempt from the text-contrast rule on the Color sheet.
