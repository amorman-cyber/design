The one rule people break
YL Green on white is 2.05:1 — it is not readable as text at any size. Green is a fill. When you need green words, use Deep Green #4E7A12. When you need words on green, use Ink.
Proportion, roughly 70 / 25 / 5
Seventy percent white and paper, twenty-five percent slate, five percent green. If green is the first thing you see everywhere on the page, there is too much of it.
